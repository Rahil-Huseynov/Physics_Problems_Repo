## Problem 2 – Parametric trajectory

$$
\vec r(t) = (t^2,\sin t,5)
$$

Velocity:
$$
\vec v(t) = \frac{d\vec r}{dt} = (2t,\cos t,0)
$$

Acceleration:
$$
\vec a(t) = (2,-\sin t,0)
$$

Speed at $t=1$:
$$
|\vec v(1)| = \sqrt{4 + \cos^2 1}
$$

Dot product:
$$
\vec v \cdot \vec a = 4t - \sin t \cos t
$$

Cross product:
$$
\vec v \times \vec a = (0,0,-2t\sin t - 2\cos t)
$$
