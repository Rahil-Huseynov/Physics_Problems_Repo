## Problem 4 – Geometry of curves

### A)

$$
x=R\cos t,\quad y=R\sin t
$$

$$
x^2+y^2=R^2
$$

Velocity:
$$
\vec v=(-R\sin t,R\cos t)
$$

$$
|\vec v|=R
$$

---

### B)

$$
x=a\cos t,\quad y=b\sin t
$$

$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1
$$

Ellipse.

---

### C)

$$
x=t,\quad y=t^2
$$

$$
y=x^2
$$

Parabola.

---

### D)

$$
x=\cosh t,\quad y=\sinh t
$$

$$
x^2-y^2=1
$$

Hyperbola.
