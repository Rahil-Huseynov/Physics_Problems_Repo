## Problem 3 – Integration of motion

### A)

$$
\vec v(t) = (2t,3,-e^{-t}), \quad \vec r(0)=(0,1,2)
$$

$$
\vec r(t) = \vec r(0) + \int_0^t \vec v(\tau)d\tau
$$

$$
= (0,1,2) + (t^2,3t,e^{-t}-1)
$$

$$
= (t^2,1+3t,1+e^{-t})
$$

Acceleration:
$$
\vec a(t) = (2,0,e^{-t})
$$

### B)

$$
\vec a(t) = (4,-\sin t,0)

$$
\vec v(t) = (1,0,2) + (4t,1-\cos t,0)

$$
\vec r(t) = (0,0,0) + \int_0^t \vec v(\tau)d\tau
